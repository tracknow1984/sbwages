Australian place coordinates: GeoNames postal code dataset.
Source: https://download.geonames.org/export/zip/AU.zip
Downloaded: 23 October 2026. License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
All 17,522 named Australian place/state records are included; this is not a customer extract.
The source's estimated place coordinates are converted to a JSON mapping, gzip-compressed
and base64-encoded in au_places.json.gz.b64. Client records are matched locally at runtime.
Coordinates may be approximated from neighbouring postal codes; no warranty of accuracy.
