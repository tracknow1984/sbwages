Planning assumptions & dispatch checks

Full site targets are used as exchange quantities: pallecons have been replaced one-for-one with 1100L bins. The saved truck position limit applies to each separate depot load. Each 1100L bin is provisionally one position; 240L bins use the saved footprint. Confirm actual bin dimensions, loaded weights, tailgate limits, empty-bin stock and unloading space before allocating a truck.

Travel estimates use local suburb centres, a 1.4 distance factor, average speeds of 35 km/h (metro) or 65 km/h (regional), a 25% time allowance and local access time. These are not measured truck routes or traffic forecasts. Confirm the road route, client receiving hours, public-holiday opening and ASIC access before dispatch. No client locations are sent to a mapping provider.

Allowances: 15 minutes preparation, 30 minutes break and 30 minutes waiting each collection day; 10 minutes per stop plus 3 minutes per bin exchanged; 20 minutes per depot load plus 4 minutes per bin for loading and unloading. Baling reserves 8 hours. Work above the daily or saved weekly staffing limit is flagged for extra capacity. No overtime is assumed. Interstate linehaul is separate.

Weekly and eight-week cycles begin in the week of 26 October. Fortnightly suburb groups are staggered between the weeks of 26 October and 2 November to balance workloads. Flexible monthly visits fall in the week containing the 26th; “last Monday” and “last Tuesday” follow the actual calendar month. Confirm first-cycle dates where the previous service date is unknown. Original schedules remain in the client profiles and changes there feed unsaved proposals. Saved weekly plans are controlled by the administrator and keep their approved dates until the administrator changes them.

Local geographic reference: GeoNames, CC BY 4.0, Australian postal places downloaded 23 October 2026. Coordinates may be approximate.
