Start a sample cycle

Two customer jobs, one truck and six bins are ready. Choose NSW for a Wetherill Park to Brisbane transfer. You can enter the printed bin and bale IDs in each scanner.

What to test

  1. Start Driver day, accept the stops, choose the truck and complete the prestart.
  2. Load three empties. At each stop, deliver the empties and pick up the full bins. Return to the depot and scan the three full bins off the truck.
  3. Open Warehouse & baling. Weigh and classify the returns and sign both PODs.
  4. Scan the two COTTON bins into one 125 kg bale and the PC MIX bin into one 75 kg bale. Check the three source bins are empty again.
  5. Load both bale IDs onto a transfer to the sample Brisbane depot. Dispatch, receive and sign the BlockTexx storage handover for 200 kg.

The sample uses its own records. Live allocations, wages, emails and Xero are outside this exercise.
