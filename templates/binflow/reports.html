How timings are measured
  • Load truck: loading becomes available after prestart and schedule acceptance → final empty bin loaded. Covers the whole truck load.
  • Unload at client: “I’ve arrived” → final empty bin delivered.
  • Reload at client: final empty delivery → final full bin collected. For pickup-only stops, timing starts at “I’ve arrived”.
  • Unload at depot: “I’m at the warehouse” → final full bin unloaded at that depot.

Times include waiting within each stage and exclude recorded breaks. They are workflow durations, not a measure of continuous physical work. Driving and warehouse weighing are not included.

Recording starts with this update. Older work is not backfilled. Stages already underway are marked Partial and measured from the first subsequent scan. Partial, incomplete and in-progress stages are excluded from averages. Sample runs stay separate from this report.

Times use the driver’s recorded timezone. Per-bin averages use total completed-stage time divided by total bins; compare the bin mix as well as the time. In-progress figures update when this page is refreshed.
